Who we are

Understanding your needs, since 1996

MR Technology is a family-run business based in the Western suburbs of Johannesburg. We offer our services all over Gauteng, including Pretoria and North West.

We pride ourselves on understanding our clients' needs — to them, safety and security come first. We always strive to be fair towards our clients and our employees, and to keep communication between ourselves, our clients and our employees open and honest.

Why choose us

We have proven ourselves time and time again

We have worked with insurance companies (for the past 17 years), home owners, construction companies and commercial business owners. You can ask us for references.
